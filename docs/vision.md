# Ruckus — Vision

> Tap a link. Enter a room code. Eight people, ten minutes, six rounds of nonsense.

## The Spine

> **Understand it in five seconds → survive it → laugh about it.**

Every minigame answers to that sentence. A round the party cannot understand from
one glance at the arena has failed before it starts, no matter how clever the rule
is. Ruckus is not a game about mastery; it is a game about a room full of people
reacting at the same time.

## The five pillars

1. **Legible in five seconds.** The rule is one sentence, shown before the round.
   If it needs a tutorial, it is the wrong minigame.
2. **One thumb.** A stick and at most a button. Anyone can be handed a phone
   mid-match and play the next round without instruction.
3. **Everyone watches everything.** A fixed camera, the whole arena on screen, no
   occlusion. Being eliminated is still fun because you can see what happens next.
4. **Rounds are short and losing is cheap.** Roughly thirty to sixty seconds at a
   full lobby. A bad round costs a round, never the match. *(The original figure was
   sixty to ninety, written before anything existed; measurement moved it — RD-011.
   The pillar is unchanged, only the number.)*
5. **Chaos beats balance.** Perfect fairness is not the goal; a story worth
   retelling afterwards is.

## What Ruckus is not

- **Not a progression game.** No unlocks that change power, no grind, no meta.
  Cosmetics at most, and only much later.
- **Not a skill ladder.** A first-time player must be able to win a round through
  luck. If skill dominates every round, the party stops being a party.
- **Not an install.** If joining ever requires an app store, the design has failed.
- **Not a content treadmill.** Minigames are cheap by construction; the game grows
  by adding rules, never by adding assets.

## Why this shape

Ruckus follows a hiatus on Testament, and its structure is a direct response to
what stalled there (`docs/DECISION_LOG.md`, RD-001):

- **A minigame is a whole feature.** It ships alone, is playable the night it is
  finished, and has a scope ceiling built in. Testament's specs were all slices of
  one interdependent thing, so none of them could ever be *done*.
- **The art cannot become the work.** Geometry is code and the Kit is closed, so
  there is no asset to polish for a week. Testament lost months to decorating two
  rooms while combat did not exist yet.
- **Status is derived.** Nothing about what is shipped is asserted by hand.

## The measure of a new minigame

Ask: **"Would a room of eight people, half of them drunk, half of them new, all
understand this within five seconds and be shouting by second ten?"**

If not, cut it.
