Ruckus · specs/visual-direction

Two registers

A PS1-era low-poly world rendered chunky and dithered, wrapped in a bright, chunky party interface that stays razor sharp. The contrast is the direction, not a compromise inside it.

The world

Renders to a 270-pixel-tall buffer and upscales with no smoothing. Flat shading, hard edges, 15-bit colour, ordered dither, distance fog.

480 × 270 · nearest · 5 bits/channel

The interface

DOM at native resolution, so type and panels stay crisp on a 3× phone display. Never drawn into the retro buffer.

native · subpixel type · 44px targets

Textures are code

Every texture below is generated in this page by the same functions the spec defines — no image files, no loaders, so kit_check.py stays green by construction rather than by exemption. PS1 textures were small tiling patterns, which are cheaper to write than to author and manage.

The generator kit · 64 × 64 · nearest · repeat

The limit, stated plainly. A generator cannot paint a logo, a sign or a portrait. The design does without those. The one place that looked like it needed painting — a face — turns out not to.

Eight faces, from four numbers

A PS1 face was a 32×32 texture with dot eyes and a mouth. That is a handful of drawing primitives. faceFor(slot) varies eye spacing, eye size, brow angle and mouth shape from the slot seed — so the eight players get eight different faces, which one painted texture could never do.

This is the second identity channel RD-007 named and did not build. Eight colours that survive dichromacy is over-constrained. Eight colours plus eight faces is not.

The character

Boxes, a generated face, and no rig. Footprint and PLAYER_RADIUS are unchanged from the capsule it replaces, so nothing about collision, spawning or any minigame's geometry moves — this stays a render-layer change.

Animation is a pure function, as it already was

Legs counter-rotate on a phase from speed; arms counter-swing against them; airborne poses differently from grounded. No rig, no keyframes, no clips — every angle is a function of (speed, height, grounded, t), so it is testable without a renderer.

Motion

Entrances scale from 0.86 with an overshoot and settle; scores count up rather than appearing; the round card deals in, holds, and flicks away. Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation goes and every piece of information stays — the animation is emphasis, never the message.

The palette

The eight player colours are not chosen by eye — they came out of a search against deuteranopia and protanopia simulation (RD-007), which is why two of them are deliberately dark. Brightening them uniformly re-breaks the constraint.
